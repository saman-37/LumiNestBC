# Pitch sources

Research for the LuminestBC pitch. Checked on 2026-10-03.

We looked for real-time shelter bed apps from around 2017 in San Diego, Florida, Tucson
and Vancouver, and for what happened to them. Only Vancouver had a 2017 system we could
source. The San Diego, Florida and Tucson apps we found are newer (2023 to 2026).
Where we found nothing, we say so.

## Summary

| Place | 2017 system found? | Evidence it failed because data went stale? |
|---|---|---|
| San Diego | No. The app was proposed in 2023 and launched in 2024–2025. | No source found. |
| Florida | No. Earliest we found is Pinellas County, 2025. | No source found. |
| Tucson | No. Pima County dashboard and Nexus for Hope are 2025–2026. | No direct source. Coverage is partial (730 of 881 beds). |
| Vancouver | Yes. StreetMessenger, August 2017. | No source on what happened to it. Its site did not respond when we checked. |
| Los Angeles (extra) | Bed system assigned in 2016. | **Yes.** A 2023 audit says the app had inaccurate data and few providers used it. |

**Be careful in the pitch.** We could not source the claim that the 2017 apps in
San Diego, Florida and Tucson failed. The strongest sourced example of a bed app failing
because data wasn't kept up is Los Angeles. Say "Los Angeles" or "other cities", not
those four by name.

## San Diego

No 2017 source found. Searches only turned up the Shelter Ready app, which is newer.

- **DA proposes a shelter bed app.** District Attorney Summer Stephan asked the County Board of Supervisors to fund a $300,000 app to show outreach workers which beds are open.
  [Voice of San Diego](https://voiceofsandiego.org/2023/03/15/da-stephan-pushing-new-homeless-shelter-app/), 2023-03-15.
- **Shelter Ready launches countywide.** Built by Caravan Studios (TechSoup), it lets outreach workers see beds and reserve them; a North County pilot came first.
  [GovTech](https://www.govtech.com/civic/new-app-distributes-san-diego-homeless-shelter-beds), 2025-06-09.
  Also [KPBS](https://www.kpbs.org/news/quality-of-life/2025/06/09/shelter-bed-app-launched-across-san-diego-county), 2025-06-09.
- **What happened:** still running. 10News reports it cut placement time by more than half in its first year.
  [10News](https://www.10news.com/homepage-showcase/shelter-ready-app-helping-cut-shelter-placement-time-by-more-than-half-in-the-first-year-of-use) (date not confirmed).
- No source found on problems with shelters keeping counts updated.

## Florida

No 2017 source found. Searches of Miami, Orlando, Tampa and Jacksonville found no
bed availability app from 2016 to 2018.

- **Pinellas County Community Services Mobile App.** Powered by WellSky and fed by the county HMIS, it shows outreach workers real-time shelter beds; officials called it the first time they'd had something like it.
  [Bay News 9](https://baynews9.com/fl/tampa/news/2025/10/16/homeless-shelter-app-pinellas-county), 2025-10-16.
- No source found on stale data.

## Tucson

No 2017 source found.

- **Pima County shelter bed dashboard.** Built for the Tucson Pima Collaboration to End Homelessness; providers report beds several times a day; it covers 730 of the county's 881 beds.
  [AZPM](https://news.azpm.org/p/azpmnews/2026/4/22/229407-pima-county-launches-up-to-date-unhoused-shelter-bed-database/), 2026-04-22.
- **Nexus for Hope.** A community-built app from the Tucson Crime Free Coalition showing open beds across nonprofits in real time.
  [KGUN9](https://www.kgun9.com/news/community-inspired-journalism/midtown-news/community-led-app-to-help-people-experiencing-homelessness-find-open-shelter-beds-in-real-time) (date not confirmed).
- No source found on a failed earlier system. The partial coverage (about 83% of beds) shows that not every provider takes part.

## Vancouver

- **StreetMessenger launches with live bed counts.** Vancouver Community Network's StreetMessenger let shelter staff text in their open beds, shown on a Google map, and texted updates to street-involved youth.
  [Canada Newswire press release](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/vancouvers-streetmessenger-now-displays-shelter-bed-availability-in-real-time-on-google-maps-and-expands-across-canada-to-combat-homelessness-641388123.html), 2017-08-22.
  Also [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/streetmessenger-sends-homeless-youth-real-time-updates-via-sms-on-shelter-beds/), 2017-08-22.
- **No live bed counts online for B.C.** A columnist notes the best B.C. shelter map shows only static data like total beds, never open beds, and the City's shelter list PDF was over a year old.
  [Georgia Straight](https://www.straight.com/life/990761/homeless-vancouver-shouldnt-be-so-hard-google-shelter-bed-internet), 2017-11-03.
- **What happened to StreetMessenger:** no source found. streetmessenger.ca did not respond on 2026-10-03. We don't know why or when it stopped.

## BC211 (what Metro Vancouver uses today)

- **BC211 shelter list.** The page says the list "is updated Monday to Friday (excluding stats) twice a day at 11:30 am and 7:30 pm." There are no updates overnight or on weekends.
  [bc.211.ca/shelter-lists](https://bc.211.ca/shelter-lists/), checked 2026-10-03.

## Extra: Los Angeles (best evidence for "updating was a chore")

Not one of the four cities, but this is the clearest sourced case.

- **City audit: no working bed system after seven years.** LA was told to build a bed availability system in 2016. In 2023 staff still tracked beds by phone calls and daily emails, and during storms operators had to call several shelters before each referral.
  [LAist](https://laist.com/news/housing-homelessness/finding-a-shelter-bed-in-la-isnt-easy-la-city-controller-releases-audit), 2023-12-05.
- **The app failed on data and participation.** "LAHSA's Find-a-Shelter app had inaccurate data and did not attract large participation by providers, which limited its function."
  [Los Angeles Times via Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/woefully-inadequate-why-hard-shelter-221042528.html), 2023-12-06.
