Flow A · Front Desk

The 2-Second NFC Tap Board

Inexpensive physical NFC tags right beside the shelter front desk:

  • Bed Freed (+1): Guest checks out. One tap adds a bed.
  • Bed Filled (−1): Guest arrives. One tap claims a bed.
  • Full (0): Marks shelter full immediately.
  • Arrival Tag: Confirms active 60-min hold at the door.
🛡️ Fail-Safe Engineering: Tokenized tap IDs ensure cell signal retries never double-count. A 10-second instant undo banner lets staff reverse mistaps with 1 tap.

Three Core Engineering Safeguards:

1. Zero Hallucinations:

Gemini only parses speech into JSON. Deterministic Python makes all bed match decisions.

2. Privacy by Architecture:

Zero client names stored. DV shelter coordinates and addresses are stripped at the database boundary.

3. Immutable Audit Log:

TimescaleDB hypertable logs every tap, hold, and release for regional capacity planning.
