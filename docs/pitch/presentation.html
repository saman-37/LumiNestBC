The Real-World Crisis

11:00 PM on East Hastings. Rain is falling.

The Stale Data Trap

BC211's public shelter list updates only twice a day on weekdays. Overnight and throughout weekends, the list is completely frozen.

The Blind Phone-Call Cycle

Outreach workers spend 45 to 60 minutes calling shelter after shelter while the person they are helping waits in the freezing cold.

Meanwhile, shelter staff at the front desk are fielding repetitive phone calls all night on top of managing high-stress front-line intake.

💡 The tragedy: Beds open up at 10:30 PM, but people sleep outside because nobody knows they are free.
Root Cause Analysis

Why Did Multi-Million Dollar Apps Fail?

In 2023, the Los Angeles City Controller audited their citywide shelter tracking app. The result: completely inaccurate data and zero adoption.

⌨️

High Data Entry Friction

Shelter workers don't have time to log into software, remember 12-character passwords, and fill out forms while checking someone in at 2 AM.

⏱️

No Data Freshness

A pin showing "2 beds open" without an age is useless. If a worker walks 20 minutes to find the bed taken hours ago, trust in the app is broken forever.

🏃

No Hold Mechanism

Knowing a bed exists doesn't help if another worker takes it while you're traveling. Without a hold, every search is a race.

🎯 OpenBed's Core Insight: The problem was never the map UI. The bottleneck was data entry speed.

Front Desk Experience

Flow A: The 2-Second NFC Tap Board

We put inexpensive NFC tags beside the shelter front desk register:

  • Bed Freed (+1): Guest leaves. One tap adds a bed.
  • Bed Filled (−1): Guest arrives. One tap claims a bed.
  • Full (0): Sets beds to zero instantly.
  • Door Arrival Tag: Confirms active 60-min hold on arrival.
🛡️ Real-World Engineering: Tokenized tap IDs prevent double-taps on poor connectivity. 10-second instant undo banner fixes accidental touches.

Voice & AI Architecture

Extracted Reasoning Trace: Accessible ✓ Pets OK ✓ 3 min walk updated 2m ago

ElevenLabs Spoken Narration

The top match is immediately read aloud via high-fidelity streaming audio so outreach workers on the street can listen hands-free.

Atomic 60-Min Bed Hold

Clicking "Hold Bed" performs an atomic row lock (SELECT FOR UPDATE). Count drops by 1. Zero risk of double-booking.

Three Core Engineering Safeguards:

1. Zero Hallucinations:

Gemini only converts messy speech into criteria. Deterministic Python makes all shelter matching decisions.

2. Privacy by Architecture:

Zero client names stored. DV shelter coordinates and addresses are mathematically stripped at the database boundary.

3. Immutable Audit Log:

TimescaleDB hypertable logs every tap, hold, and release for regional capacity planning.
